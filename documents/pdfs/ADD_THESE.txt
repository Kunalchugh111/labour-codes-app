All four labour Codes and their Central Rules are now processed and built in.

To replace or update a document later:
  1. Drop the new PDF into this folder (use a filename containing the
     relevant keyword: wage / industrial / social / osh, and "rule"
     for the Central Rules variants).
  2. Run:  python ingest.py
  3. Commit the new .pdf and the regenerated .txt in ../processed/.

Filenames don't need to be exact — keywords (wage / industrial / social
/ osh + "rule" for rules files) are matched automatically by ingest.py.
